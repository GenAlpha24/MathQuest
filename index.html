❓ How to Play

Goal: Run across the level, jump over spikes, and beat every monster to clear the level.

Move: Your hero runs on its own. Press Space or ↑ to jump (or tap the game on touch screens). Jump over the spikes. You can land safely on top of the gray rocks and the cactuses, just don't run straight into their side. Landing on a rock also earns a bonus life ❤️ (once per rock). Watch out for flying birds, stay on the ground to pass under them! Jump over spinning saw blades, flames, and rolling logs, and time your jumps to dodge the low-flying bats and swinging spikeballs. Watch for geysers that erupt from the ground, wait for them to rest before running past. Bounce on red springs to launch extra high, dodge rolling boulders, and run clear of falling icicles. And jump over the holes and ponds in the ground, falling in costs a life. Hitting an obstacle costs a life too.

Monster battles: When you reach a monster, a math question appears. Pick the correct answer before the timer runs out. Right answer beats the monster (and answering fast gives bonus points). A wrong answer or running out of time costs a life.

Lives: You start with 3. Clearing a whole level rewards you with +1 life. ❤️

Grade: Pick your grade on the start screen to set the math difficulty (Grades 1–5).

Pause: Press the ⏸ Pause button or the P / Esc key anytime.

Saving: Your level, grade, and best score are saved to your account automatically.

⏸ Paused

Take a break! Your progress is safe. Press Resume (or the P key) to keep playing.
