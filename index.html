❓ How to Play

Goal: Run across the level, jump over spikes, and beat every monster to clear the level.

Move: Your hero runs on its own. Press Space or ↑ to jump (or tap the game on touch screens). Jump over the spikes. You can land safely on top of the gray rocks and the cactuses, just don't run straight into their side. Watch out for flying birds, stay on the ground to pass under them! Jump over spinning saw blades, flames, and rolling logs, and time your jumps to dodge the low-flying bats and swinging spikeballs. Watch for geysers that erupt from the ground, wait for them to rest before running past. Bounce on red springs to launch extra high, dodge rolling boulders, and run clear of falling icicles. Jump over the green poisonous mist, don't breathe it! Hop over the wiggly worms, the slow snails 🐌, the big bears, slithering snakes, buzzing beehives, and bubbling slime puddles too. And jump over the holes and ponds in the ground, falling in costs a life. Hitting an obstacle costs a life too.

Monster battles: When you reach a monster, a math question appears. Pick the correct answer before the timer runs out, either tap a choice, or press T to jump to the type box, type your answer, and press Enter. Every correct answer earns you a life ❤️ (and answering fast gives bonus points), and 5 correct answers in a row earns a bonus life! 🔥 A wrong answer or running out of time costs a life and breaks your streak.

Lives: You start with 3. Clearing a whole level rewards you with +1 life, and grabbing a gem 💎 gives +1 life too. ❤️

Treasure chests: Grab a chest 🎁 for a surprise advantage, +5 lives, a shield 🛡 that blocks your next hit, extra time on upcoming questions ⏱, or bonus points ⭐. Chests can also hold these perks (active ones show in a little bar at the top left, under the message line):

  • 🎯 50/50: two wrong answers vanish on your next 2 questions (store up to 4).
  • ⏭ Free Skip: tap the Free Skip button in a battle to pass a question with no penalty (no points or life either). Store up to 2.
  • ✖️2 Double Points: your next 3 correct answers earn double points (store up to 10).
  • 🧲 Gem Magnet: gems nearby fly to you for 10 seconds. It ends when the level does.
  • 🐌 Slow-Mo: the hero runs slower for 5 seconds, so it's easier to time jumps.
  • 🦘 Super Jump: your next 4 jumps go higher (store up to 8).
  • 🔥 Streak Saver: your answer streak survives your next mistake (you still lose the life). Store up to 2.

Timed perks pause when you pause or fight a monster. Lucky perks you haven't used stay with you through levels, but are cleared when a new run starts or the game ends.

Mega gem: Collect most of the gems in a level and a rainbow mega-rare gem ✨ appears near the finish, a mega gem is worth 4 gems (+4 lives)! And if you reach the end having collected most of the gems, you earn the mega gem anyway as a bonus. You also earn a mega gem each time you stockpile lots of lives (every 10 lives). Your mega gem count is shown in the HUD.

Ultra gem: Stockpile a huge pile of lives and you earn a super-rare ultra gem 🌟, worth 8 gems (+8 lives)! You earn one ultra gem for every 30 lives (at 30, 60, 90, and so on), and your ultra gem count is shown in the HUD.

Grade: Pick your grade on the start screen to set the math difficulty (Grades 1–5).

Pause: Press the ⏸ Pause button or the P / Esc key anytime.

Saving: Your level, grade, and best score are saved to your account automatically.

⏸ Paused

Take a break! Your progress is safe. Press Resume (or the P key) to keep playing.
